Getting a brand-new product found on Google

The brief
Headout launched its own city pass: one ticket that covers several attractions in a city. Nobody was searching for it by name yet, and it had no pages built for search.
What I did
Worked out which cities and searches mattered most, built 16 landing pages and 4 reusable page templates, and linked to them from pages that already ranked well. Within six months the pages were ranking in London, Barcelona, Vienna, Dubai and New York.

How I work

newsroom habits, growth goals

Five habits that run through everything on this page, from Metrolife to Headout.

  1. 01

    Start with the reader

    Before writing anything, find out what people are actually searching for and asking. It's the same instinct as finding the story in a newsroom.

    Seen in: Metrolife trend stories, the AI search work
  2. 02

    Let the data set priorities

    Rank the work by revenue, demand, competition and season, so the team spends time where it pays off.

    Seen in: a growth plan built from 457 pages
  3. 03

    Build it once, reuse it often

    Turn every solved problem into a playbook, template or prompt the next person can pick up.

    Seen in: playbooks for 9 product categories
  4. 04

    Keep an editor's eye on AI

    Automation makes content fast. Editing makes it worth reading, so the quality rules go into the system from day one.

    Seen in: city launches in 30 minutes, in the brand's voice
  5. 05

    Grow the people

    Hire well, train with patience and give people room to take on more.

    Seen in: up to 10 writers mentored, with promotions along the way
